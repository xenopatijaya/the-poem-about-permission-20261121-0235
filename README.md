# The poem about permission

A short poem about agency — the power to ask and to be given — written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235).

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). The run is deterministic: the same poem on every run.

## The poem

> The Chalk
> Mid-sentence I stopped, chalk dust on my fingers,
> and held it out to the quiet girl in the second row.
> She took it, and the board, which had been mine,
> became a question she could answer in her own hand.
> I had taught her the sums. She taught me the asking.

One concrete moment — a teacher handing the chalk to a student mid-lesson — and the turn it carries: the board stops being the teacher's and becomes a question the student can answer in her own hand, and the teacher discovers that agency is taught by giving it away, not by keeping it.
